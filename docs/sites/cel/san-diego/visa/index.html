F-1 Visa

How to Apply for an F-1 Visa with CEL San Diego

CEL San Diego issues the Form I-20 for full-time English courses. After enrolling, you use it to pay the SEVIS fee, complete the DS-160 and apply for your F-1 visa at a US embassy. Tuition is not due until your visa is issued. Most students start 3–5 months before their course.

General information, not legal advice — always verify current rules at travel.state.gov.

Which Visa

Is an F-1 Visa Right for Your CEL Course?

CEL courses with 24 or more lessons per week are full-time programs and require an F-1 student visa for international students who need a US study visa. For English-language training, official US guidance defines a full course of study as at least 18 clock hours per week of mostly classroom instruction — at CEL, that's 24 lessons.

Below that level, GE20 and GE23 are part-time — and many students take them as part of a visit to the US: short, non-credit study is permitted on ESTA (eligible nationalities) and on a B1/B2 visitor visa. Whether a visitor route fits depends on the purpose and length of your trip and your circumstances — not on weekly hours alone. Not sure which fits your plans? Read the tourist-vs-student visa guide — or just tell us your plans and we'll help you check.

Step by Step

The Process: What You Do and What CEL Does

You never carry this process alone. Here is who does what, at every stage:

  1. Step 1

    Enroll

    What you do

    Send your documents and pay the registration fee

    What CEL does

    Confirms your enrollment

  2. Step 2

    Form I-20

    What you do

    Check every detail on the draft — name, dates, course

    What CEL does

    Prepares your I-20 and sends a draft by email, usually within 1–2 business days of complete documents; issues the final I-20 after your confirmation

  3. Step 3

    Visa application

    What you do

    Pay the SEVIS I-901 fee at fmjfee.com, complete the DS-160, book and attend your embassy interview — wait times vary widely by country

    What CEL does

    Provides school documentation and support letters

  4. Step 4

    After approval

    What you do

    Pay tuition and book your travel — new F-1 students may generally seek entry up to 30 days before the I-20 start date (cbp.gov)

    What CEL does

    Confirms your booking and prepares your arrival

  5. Step 5

    Arrival

    What you do

    Your first Monday at CEL, 945 Hornblend Street, Pacific Beach

    What CEL does

    Welcomes you and gets you started

CEL usually issues the Form I-20 within 1–2 business days of receiving the registration fee and complete documents.

Visa Interview

The Embassy Interview

Interview day

What to Bring to Your Interview

  • Passport

    Generally valid at least 6 months beyond your intended stay (some nationalities are exempt — check your embassy's checklist)

  • Form I-20

    Your final version, printed and signed

  • SEVIS fee receipt

    Proof you paid the I-901 fee

  • DS-160 confirmation

    The confirmation page from your online application

  • Financial evidence

    Bank statements, sponsor confirmation

  • Ties to home

    Evidence that you plan to return home after your studies

What Do Officers Look For?

Applicants generally need to show that their study plan is credible, that they can fund their stay, and that they intend to leave the United States after their authorized studies.

Can't get an appointment before your course starts? CEL can defer your start date — see the FAQ.

One thing to know: an I-20 confirms your enrollment at CEL — it does not guarantee that a visa will be issued or that you will be admitted to the United States. That decision rests with the embassy and, on arrival, with US border officials.

If a visa is denied, reapplication is possible with stronger documentation. And on the money question: see what happens to your payments.

Official interview information and wait times: travel.state.gov

Arrival

Arriving in the USA

New F-1 students may generally seek entry to the United States up to 30 days before the program start date on their Form I-20. Final admission is decided by US Customs and Border Protection (cbp.gov).

Keep your documents — passport, I-20, SEVIS receipt — in your hand luggage. You will be admitted as "D/S" (Duration of Status) — what that means.

Students who complete their program and maintain valid F-1 status generally have a 60-day grace period after the program end date.

Transfers

Transfers & Staying in Status

Already in the USA on an F-1? CEL San Diego accepts eligible SEVIS transfers. You need:

  • The same documents as a new student

  • Your current active I-20

  • A transfer form completed by your current school (the "transfer clearance")

Minimum booking: 4 weeks.

Glossary

F-1 Terms Explained

Term

Meaning

Form I-20

The school document you need before applying for an F-1 visa — CEL issues it after enrollment

SEVP

The US program that certifies schools to enroll F-1 students

SEVIS

The US government system that stores international-student records

I-901 fee

The SEVIS fee ($350) paid online before your visa interview

DS-160

The online visa application form

DSO

The school official authorized to manage F-1 student records

D/S (Duration of Status)

The notation on your I-94 admission record. It means you may stay for your authorized period of study plus related permitted periods, as long as you maintain valid F-1 status

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CEL San Diego SEVP-certified?

+

Yes — CEL San Diego is certified to enroll F-1 students and issues the Form I-20.

Which CEL San Diego courses require an F-1 visa?

+

Courses with 24 or more lessons per week: General English 24, General English 28, and English Plus electives booked at that intensity.

Can I study at CEL without an F-1?

+

Yes, in many cases. GE20 and GE23 are part-time courses below the 18-clock-hour F-1 threshold, and many students take them while visiting the US — short, non-credit study is permitted on ESTA (eligible nationalities) and on a B1/B2 visitor visa. Whether a visitor route fits depends on the purpose and length of your trip, not on weekly hours alone. Which visa fits you? Read the guide.

What happens if my visa is denied — do I get my money back?

+

CEL does not require tuition payment until your visa is issued — so no tuition is paid before the decision. The CEL registration fee is non-refundable; government fees are normally non-refundable; accommodation cancellation fees may apply if you booked before approval.

Can I transfer to CEL from another US school on my F-1?

+

Yes — CEL San Diego accepts eligible SEVIS transfers. You need the same documents as a new student, plus your current active I-20 and a transfer form from your current school. Minimum booking: 4 weeks.

How quickly does CEL issue the I-20?

+

Usually within 1–2 business days once your registration fee and complete documents have arrived — you receive a draft to check before the final I-20 is issued.

What if I can't get a visa appointment before my course starts?

+

CEL defers your start date once, free of charge, before your course begins — further date changes are $75. You receive a revised I-20 with the new dates, usually within 1–2 business days. Accommodation booked through CEL moves free of charge with 4 or more weeks' notice.

Can my spouse or children come with me?

+

Yes — CEL San Diego issues F-2 dependant I-20s for spouses and children. Each dependant requires an additional registration fee, and your bank statement must show an extra $750 per month per dependant on top of the standard amounts.
