Frequently Asked Questions

What companies ask about fit

Which industries does Afissio work with?

Software and AI, hardware and robotics, manufacturing, logistics and supply chain, medical devices and healthcare, professional services, and real estate.

What size of company is Afissio built for?

Companies with approximately $5 million to $50 million in enterprise value that are preparing for institutional scrutiny within the next three years.

Does the review change by industry?

The facts differ by industry, but the objective is consistent: identify the issues most likely to create friction, understand their consequences, and act while there is still time to protect leverage.

Why is diligence harder in technical businesses?

Diligence reaches past contracts into intellectual property, data practices, product and technical documentation, and those demands often overlap with the commercial and financial record.

Our industry is not listed. Can we still talk?

Yes. These six are where Afissio works most often. If your company is preparing for institutional scrutiny, start the conversation and we will tell you plainly whether the review fits.

When should a company in these industries start?

Before a process becomes urgent, while careful preparation can still protect value and leverage.
