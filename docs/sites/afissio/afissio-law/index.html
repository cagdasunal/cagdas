Frequently Asked Questions

What companies ask about Afissio Law

Do we have to use Afissio Law?

No. Some findings need no legal work at all, and where one does, the company’s existing counsel can perform it.

Can Afissio Law work alongside our existing counsel?

Yes. Afissio Law can be engaged for a defined piece of legal implementation while the company’s existing advisors continue in their roles.

Is legal work included in the Review?

No. The Review identifies and prioritizes transaction-readiness issues. Any legal work needed to resolve them is engaged separately.

When does an attorney-client relationship with Afissio Law begin?

Only when the company engages Afissio Law directly for legal services and the engagement has been confirmed.

A conversation comes first

If a finding may require legal work, talk through the issue, its timing, and who should implement it.
