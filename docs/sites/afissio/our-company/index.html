About Afissio

Experienced judgment. Direct involvement. Practical preparation.

Afissio is a transaction-readiness firm built for the period before a consequential transaction becomes urgent.

Why Afissio exists

Founder-led companies are often strong in product, technology, and market execution while their documented business has received less attention. Afissio helps leadership prepare the operative record for the scrutiny that accompanies serious transactions.

Jerry Stephen Smith

CEO  /  Principal

Jerry Stephen Smith leads Afissio and is directly involved in the transaction-readiness and diligence process. His role emphasizes careful review, structured analysis, practical judgment, and close collaboration with company leadership.

Heidi Abdul

Senior Advisor

Heidi Abdul focuses on the organizational and compliance side of transaction readiness. Her background spans entity formation, governance, and organizational documents; capitalization records and complex cap tables; corporate compliance programs and required corporate filings; and transaction documentation, including diligence and disclosure schedules through closing. She has directed compliance functions in highly regulated, fast-moving environments.

The network

A network of experts

Afissio draws on a network of financial and technical experts with deep experience in the areas that matter most to transaction readiness.

On the financial side, that experience spans capitalization, operating records, financial documentation, and transaction-readiness priorities. On the technical side, it spans software, hardware, data, privacy, security, and the use of emerging technologies. These professionals are engaged as a company's circumstances require, and their work supports the same review, prioritization, and judgment that guide every Afissio engagement.
