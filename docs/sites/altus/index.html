About ALTUS

Thirty years of getting businesses paid.

ALTUS is a commercial collections firm, 300 people strong, working across the United States and Canada. The method has stayed the same for three decades: know the industry, follow the law, write everything down, and treat every debtor as a business our client may sell to again. It is a slower way to collect. It is also why clients stay.
