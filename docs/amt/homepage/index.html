Why this matters

The world is a lot for a child. And you’re the one in the room with them.

Around one in seven children and adolescents lives with a mental health condition — in the European Union alone, more than 11 million young people. Anxiety, depression and behavioural difficulties are among the leading causes of illness and disability during adolescence.

Behind every statistic is a child trying to cope with pressure, uncertainty and a world that rarely slows down. The adults around them can make a lasting difference — by helping children notice what they feel, respond more consciously and recover from hard moments, we give resilience room to grow.

“Mindfulness supports and strengthens learning, emotional intelligence and a general sense of wellbeing throughout our entire lives.”
Jon Kabat-Zinn
